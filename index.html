🎾 Padel Brain

Read the glass like a pro. Padel Brain is a rebound trainer for padel players: a virtual ball machine fires balls at the glass, and you predict where they land. Real physics, four levels, no ads, no account.

FAQ

Does the app need an account or internet?
No. Everything runs locally on your device.

Why does the ball bounce differently with spin?
Because it does in real padel too — topspin climbs the back glass, backspin dies flat. The app simulates this with real physics (Magnus effect and spin-wall coupling).

Is my data collected?
No. See the privacy policy.
